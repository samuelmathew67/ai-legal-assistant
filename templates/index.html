Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS 2023) vs IPC 1860 Transition Guide

On July 1, 2024, the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS 2023) came into effect in India, replacing the Indian Penal Code (IPC 1860). Below is a quick comparative reference of the most frequently cited criminal provisions:

Offence / Subject Matter Old Law (IPC 1860) New Law (BNS 2023) Key Changes / Punishment Note
Murder Section 302 Section 103(1) Death or life imprisonment + fine. Section 103(2) introduces mob lynching provisions.
Attempt to Murder Section 307 Section 109 Imprisonment up to 10 years + fine; life imprisonment if hurt is caused.
Theft Section 378 / 379 Section 303(1) & (2) Community service introduced for petty theft under ₹5,000 upon return of property.
Cheating & Fraud Section 415 / 420 Section 318(1) & (4) Imprisonment up to 7 years + fine for cheating and dishonestly inducing delivery of property.
Criminal Breach of Trust Section 405 / 406 Section 316 Imprisonment up to 5 years or fine, or both.
Rape & Sexual Offences Section 375 / 376 Section 63 / 64 Rigorous imprisonment not less than 10 years extending to life. Section 69 penalizes deceitful promises to marry.
Cruelty by Husband / Relatives Section 498A Section 85 & 86 Imprisonment up to 3 years + fine. Cruelty definition retained and clarified.
Defamation Section 499 / 500 Section 356 Simple imprisonment up to 2 years, fine, or community service.
Criminal Intimidation Section 503 / 506 Section 351 Imprisonment up to 2 years or fine; up to 7 years if threat to cause death or grievous hurt.
New Criminal Code

Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023

The principal substantive criminal law of India replacing the IPC. Comprises 358 sections, introducing community service, organized crime penalties, cyber-crime coordination, and revised chapters.

Effective Date: July 1, 2024
Transitional & Historical

Indian Penal Code, 1860

The historic 575-section criminal code. Essential for ongoing trials, prior offences committed before July 2024, and cross-reference research across judicial precedents.

Coverage: All 575 Sections Indexed
Cyber Law & Digital Crimes

Information Technology Act, 2000

Covers cyber security breaches, hacking (Sec 66), identity theft (Sec 66C), UPI personation and phishing fraud (Sec 66D), privacy violations (Sec 66E), and electronic obscenity (Sec 67).

Redressal: National Cyber Portal (1930)
